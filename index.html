CIXD 3110/6110 · Fall 2026 · Midterm

The whole course, taken apart.把整门课拆开来看。

This term we treated AI not as magic but as a stack of things you can take apart: an economy that runs on our behavior, old ways of organizing knowledge, models made of numbers, data and human labor, and the products, agents and guidelines built on top.

We traced a URL, knolled a taxonomy, watched a network learn to draw a line, typed one prompt into four products, built a vault an agent can read, ran models on our own laptops and trained an adapter. The point was to understand each layer well enough to decide, as designers, what to do with it.

这学期我们没把 AI 当成魔法，而是当成一层层可以拆开的东西：靠我们的行为运转的经济、古老的知识组织方式、由数字、数据和人的劳动构成的模型，以及建在它们之上的产品、智能体和设计准则。

我们追踪过一个网址、把物品规整排列成分类、看一个神经网络学会画分界线、把同一个提示词输入四个产品、建了一个智能体能读的笔记库、在自己的电脑上跑模型，还训练了一个适配器。目的是把每一层都弄懂，作为设计师，才能决定拿它来做什么。

ZZINE

The printed zine — a sample打印版小册子（zine）范例

The exam can also be handed in as a printed zine: print the pages with the zine button on the exam page, cut and paste or hand-write your answers, add drawings or collage, and clip it with the binder clips provided. Below is the whole thing done that way — every question, a drawing, and the full-credit answer in handwriting, with the key words highlighted. The ten pages with captions show a hand-made technique you can copy with pen, scissors, tape, highlighter and sticky notes.

考试也可以交打印版小册子：用考卷页面顶部的 zine 按钮打印，把答案剪贴上去或手写，加上手绘图或拼贴，再用老师发的长尾夹夹好。下面就是按这个方式做好的完整一本——每道题都有一张图和手写的得分版答案，关键词用荧光笔标出。带说明的十页各示范一种手工做法，用笔、剪刀、胶带、荧光笔和便利贴就能做出来。
